# myway Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-07-17**

myway is a browser extension that provides keyboard shortcuts for navigating web pages. Your
privacy is fundamental to this extension. This policy explains what data myway does and does
not collect.

## Summary

**myway does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.** All of your settings
and customization are stored locally in your browser.

## What data myway stores, and where

myway uses your browser's built-in extension storage (`chrome.storage.local` and
`chrome.storage.session`). This data never leaves your device.

| Data | Purpose | Stored where |
|---|---|---|
| Your custom key mappings, exclusions, options | To remember your preferences | Local browser storage |
| Find-mode search history (within a page) | To provide find-next/previous | Local browser storage |
| User-defined link-hint CSS | To apply your styling | Local browser storage |

## What myway does NOT do

- myway **does not** collect analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics.
- myway **does not** connect to any server owned by the developer. The only network activity is
  what *you* initiate — e.g. when you use a Vomnibar search shortcut to open a search engine.
- myway **does not** read, transmit, or store the content of the pages you visit. Content
  scripts run locally in your tab only to enable keyboard navigation.
- myway **does not** include any third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.

myway is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
